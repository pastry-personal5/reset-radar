# Reset Radar

Reset Radar monitors AI-service usage and reset times across your MacBook, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

*See what’s left. Know when it resets.*

The product is planned for personal use across the owner's Apple devices. A Mac collector automatically reads derived coding-allowance state from installed first-party Codex and Claude Code tools; iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch present percentage used, reset times, freshness, and platform-appropriate glanceable surfaces. An iPhone or iPad user can also request a fresh Codex reading from an awake Mac. Forget Mac hides local iPhone data at once, completes local secret and surface cleanup, and reports Watch erasure as pending until the responding Watch confirms its cache is cleared; the Watch also offers a local erase action. An owner-operated Cloudflare relay is conditionally approved as the encrypted store-and-forward transport, subject to its synthetic-data architecture spike. The Apple apps are signed with a free Apple account and installed for personal use, with no App Store or TestFlight distribution planned. See the [product behavior](docs/product-behavior.md), [owner decisions](docs/phase-1/owner-decisions.md), [automatic acquisition research](docs/research/phase-1/automatic-usage-acquisition.md), [Cloudflare synchronization research](docs/research/phase-1/cloudflare-synchronization.md), and [sideloading research](docs/research/phase-1/personal-use-sideloading.md).
